Session Highlights

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Our students embarked on an exciting educational trip to the Train Museum in Lagos State. It was a hands-on learning experience that brought classroom knowledge to life and deepened their understanding of transportation and infrastructure in our country.

WAEC Approval – A Big Win!

We are thrilled to announce that our secondary school has been officially approved to conduct the WAEC examination. Our first sitting was held between May and June. This milestone opens new doors of opportunity for our students' academic future.

CBT Introduction

We proudly launched our Computer-Based Testing (CBT) system this session with outstanding success. Both students and teachers adapted quickly to the new format, setting a strong foundation for modern assessment.

Colour Week Celebration

Our Preschool, Pre-Nursery, and Nursery sections enjoyed a vibrant Colour Week. Each day featured different themed colors with fun activities and expressive arts that made the week both educational and exciting.

Inter-Class Competition

Students showcased their talents in academics, sports, and creative challenges. This event fostered healthy rivalry, teamwork, and confidence among all participants.
