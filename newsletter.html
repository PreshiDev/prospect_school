First Term, 2025/2026 Session

Dear Parents and Guardians,

As we conclude the First Term of the 2025/2026 academic session, we give all glory to God for His unfailing mercy, guidance, and abundant grace. He has remained our strength, our helper, and our constant source of support. We also extend our heartfelt appreciation to our wonderful parents and guardians for your continuous cooperation, encouragement, and trust in our school.

We equally appreciate our dedicated teachers for their tireless efforts, commitment, and passion in nurturing our learners and ensuring academic excellence throughout the term.

This term has been marked by growth, progress, and remarkable achievements for the management, staff, and our learners across all levels.

During The Term

Inter-Class Competitions

Students across all levels actively participated in various inter-class academic competitions, including Mathematics Quiz, Spelling Bee, Debate, and other skill-building activities designed to enhance teamwork, confidence, and academic excellence.

Introduction of Class Platforms

Class WhatsApp platforms were introduced for smoother communication between teachers and parents. This has improved follow-up on assignments, announcements, and overall academic monitoring.

Career Choice Seminar

Students received guidance on selecting appropriate subjects and pathways for their future careers.

Excursion to the Indomie Factory

The excursion gave students a hands-on learning experience, exposing them to industrial processes, hygiene practices, and modern packaging techniques.

Independence Day Rally

Students proudly represented the school during the Independence Day rally, participating in interviews and showcasing discipline, patriotism, and confidence.

Excellent WAEC Results

This session marked a milestone—our first-ever WAEC examination conducted within the school premises. Although students have written WAEC for years, this was the inaugural in-house sitting. The results were outstanding, and the processing of students into tertiary institutions is currently ongoing.

Food Competition

Students showcased their creativity during the inter-class food competition, preparing different cuisines and demonstrating impressive culinary skills.

Introduction of CBT Exams

The school fully integrated Computer-Based Testing (CBT) as part of its assessment system. This innovation has improved speed, accuracy, and record-keeping, while preparing students for modern exams such as the WAEC CBT format.

Christmas Carol

The term ended on a joyful note with a beautiful Christmas Carol event, filled with music, choreography, and festive presentations.

School Club Enrollment

All students are encouraged to join one of the following clubs as part of our extracurricular development program:

Art Club

The Art Club, newly introduced this term, has been one of the most active and impactful clubs in the school. Pupils and students engage in hands-on creative activities such as paper mâché, drawing, painting, and other practical projects. A major achievement this term is the beautiful mural painted on the school wall—designed and executed entirely by Art Club members. The club continues to nurture creativity, imagination, and confidence in artistic expression.

Important Notice: Parents are kindly informed that beginning from next term, pupils and students enrolled in the Art Club will be required to make a small contribution to support practical activities such as tie-and-dye, batik, paper crafts, and other creative projects. The exact amount will be communicated in due time.

Coding Club

Coding has become an essential skill for every 21st-century learner. In our school, Coding classes help pupils and students develop digital literacy, logical thinking, and problem-solving abilities. This program is especially important as future WAEC examinations will be conducted using CBT. Parents are strongly encouraged to ensure their children participate so they can gain the technological skills necessary for academic success.

Music Club

The Music program allows students to explore various musical instruments, develop rhythm, and improve their listening and coordination skills. Through singing, instrumental training, and performance practice, pupils enhance their creativity and self-expression. Music continues to play a key role in building confidence and enriching the cultural life of the school.

Digitalized ID Card Implementation

Important Announcement

Parents are hereby informed that beginning next term, the school will fully implement the use of Digitalized ID Cards for all pupils and students. A scanning machine will be installed at the school entrance, and students will be required to scan their ID cards before entering the school premises each day.

  • The cost of the Digitalized ID Card is ₦5,000.
  • Parents who have already paid ₦2,000 will be required to pay an additional ₦3,000 to complete the payment.

Please note that this payment is compulsory, as the ID card will be used for security, attendance tracking, and proper documentation.
