News-Letter

APPRECIATION: With great joy, we announce the conclusion of the 1st term of the 2024/2025 academic session. We give all glory to God, and may His name alone be praised. We also express our sincere gratitude for your unwavering support towards both the children and the school. It is truly wonderful to witness the cooperation as we work together to nurture and shape the future generation. A special welcome goes out to the new parents joining the Great Prospect family for the first time. You’ve made the best choice in entrusting your children to us, and we assure you that you will not regret it. Welcome!
ACADEMIC DEVELOPMENT: Academic activities this term have been a blend of theoretical and practical learning. We have incorporated skill acquisition activities such as bead-making, painting, baking, music, and coding into our curriculum to ensure that students acquire valuable skills that will help them achieve their dreams beyond school. Additionally, extra lessons and interventions have been organized to support students who need help with various subjects. We also offer early morning drill classes for terminal classes. Please note that our pass mark is 60%, meaning every student must score at least 60% in all subjects. For promotion to the next class, students in Basic 1-9 must achieve a minimum of 65%, including in Mathematics and English. Senior secondary students must achieve a minimum of 60% to progress.
ATTENDANCE & LEARNING ACTIVITIES: A gentle reminder that school starts at 7:30am each day. Any student arriving after this time will be considered to have missed half of the school day. Learning activities run from 8:00am to 3:30pm (Monday-Thursday) and 1:30pm on Fridays. If your child will be late or absent, please inform the coordinator or admin at: 09037682707 or 08039250744.
UNIFORM: It is now compulsory for all pupils, from Playgroup to Basic classes, to wear the school uniform. We do not permit replica uniforms. We ask that all pupils take pride in their appearance by wearing the correct uniform, black shoes, school-customized navy blue socks, and canvas shoes for sports.
Please ensure that your child's uniforms are in good condition and that they are prepared with all necessary school supplies for the new term.
FOOD SERVICE: We appreciate parents who consistently prepare healthy meals for our students. The importance of fruits and water cannot be overemphasized, and we encourage including fruit in your child’s meal. However, please ensure your child has breakfast at home before coming to school. Starting from the 2nd term, food service will be available at the school, with prices starting at 500 Naira.
SICKNESS/HEALTH CHALLENGES: A child who is unwell should not be sent to school; instead, they should receive proper medical care. Kindly inform the school whenever your child is absent due to health reasons.
ASSESSMENT/ASSIGNMENT & MID-TERM PROJECT: Please note that student evaluations will be based on mid-term assessments, class assignments, computer-based tests (CBT), and the end-of-term examination. We encourage you to support your child in taking these exercises seriously. Students must complete their notes, submit assignments and mid-term projects on time; failure to do so will result in disciplinary actions.
RESULT PORTAL: In line with our commitment to technological advancement, we have developed a school portal where students can access their results. Additionally, we have incorporated computer-based testing (CBT), making it essential for all students to participate in the coding class.
Note: The 1st term results are now available on the portal. Parents can access their child’s results and billing information using their generated username and password.
DISCIPLINE: The school will enforce discipline for any student who violates the school's rules and regulations, such as wearing incorrect uniforms, inappropriate hairstyles, late assignment submissions, failing to submit project work, engaging in quarrels or fights, or displaying arrogance. These measures are in place to ensure that each child receives the proper home and moral training, as our students represent their families.


    SOCIAL ACTIVITIES:
  • International Mother Tongue Day: Every 21st of February, we celebrate International Mother Tongue Day. Each student is required to pay N5, 000 to make the event colorful and meaningful.
  • Excursion: In 2025, we will have an exciting excursion with a train ride to Lagos. The experience will cost N15, 000 per student, and we look forward to seeing all students join us.
    SPECIAL ACTIVITIES:
  1. Cultural Day: A day to celebrate and showcase our diverse cultural heritage. More details will be shared later.
  2. Excursion – Train Ride: Students will embark on an exciting train ride excursion. Further details, including the date, will be communicated soon.
  3. Giving Day: Students are encouraged to bring tangible gifts to school for donation to an orphanage. Let's nurture the spirit of generosity in our children. Giving Day is observed every 14th of February, and as a school, we visit orphanages to spread love.

NURSERY/PRE-SCHOOL ACTIVITIES (Colour Week): As a Montessori school, we promote self-directed learning that fosters self-confidence, independence, critical thinking, and creativity. To enhance this, we have organized a Colour Week, where students will engage in painting, color matching, and sorting activities. The cost is N10,000.
WAEC and BECE Registration: Registration is currently ongoing for WAEC and BECE. Please ensure that your child completes the process before the deadline.

    SPLIT TESTS AND ASSESSMENTS: Starting from the second term, tests will be divided into the following sections:
  • 4th Week: Project work submission
  • 6th Week: Note checking and assessment
  • 7th Week: Midterm test Midterm break will follow immediately after the midterm test. If there are any changes to the midterm break, they will be communicated in advance.

    CLASS ASSESSMENT BREAKDOWN: The continuous assessment marks for the term will be distributed as follows:
  • Assignments: 5%
  • Presentation/Project: 5%
  • Class Attendance: 5%
  • Note Checking/Assessment: 5%
  • Midterm Test: 20%
  • Examination: 60%
  • Total: 100% we encourage all students to work diligently to secure their marks in each category.

BIRTHDAY PACKAGE: We are excited to announce a fantastic new initiative for our students! To ensure that no child is deprived of celebrating their birthday at school, we have introduced a variety of birthday celebration packages.
CODING CLASS: Our coding classes have become even more engaging, with students making significant progress. The class is now a two-day program, reflecting our commitment to exposing students to the world of technology. We strongly encourage all parents to allow their child(ren) to benefit from this valuable experience. The class fee is N10,000. We are proud to share that some of our students in this class have even created their own websites!
MUSICAL CLASS: In our musical class, students actively participate in a variety of activities designed to help them develop musical skills and knowledge.
E-NOTE: We are excited to introduce the E-Note system to improve teaching and learning efficiency. With just N10,000, each child will receive their own copy.

IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENTS
SCHOOL FEES: We express our heartfelt gratitude to all parents for the prompt payment of school fees this term. May God continue to bless you and enrich your sources of income. Timely payment is crucial for the smooth running of our great institution. Please ensure payments are made two weeks prior to resumption.
To ensure the smooth operation of the school, we kindly remind parents and guardians to settle school fees before the resumption date. Note that there will be school drives on the first day of resumption for any pupil/student with an outstanding payment.
  1. New Payment System: Starting from the second term, all payments (school fees, extra-curricular program fees, etc.) can be made directly through the school's online portal, offering a secure and convenient method for managing financial transactions.
  2. Second Term Resumption Date: The second term will begin on January 6th, 2025. We look forward to welcoming our students back for another term of growth and learning.
  3. Parental Involvement: We encourage parents to remain actively involved in their child’s education. Attend PTA meetings, monitor homework, and engage with teachers to create a collaborative environment that benefits our students.

Gratitude and Commitment: We sincerely thank all our parents for being a vital part of the Prospect Private School family. Together, we are shaping a future where every child thrives academically, socially, and emotionally. Let’s continue our journey of excellence as we approach the second term.
For any inquiries or assistance, feel free to contact us via the school office or our dedicated WhatsApp line, accessible through the contact button on our website.

HOLIDAY / RESUMPTION: We officially announce the end of the 1st term of the 2024/2025 session. The second term will commence on January 6th, 2025.
